# GLOSSARY — the MiLP page

The page is read on a phone, by the four players and anyone they show it to. These are the words it uses.

## What the page shows

- **page** — the whole site. One line, then a few sections; it is not a deck and nothing on it is called a card.
- **section** — a heading and the thing under it. The reader may skip any section; only the first one is required reading.
- **two of you** — the reader and their partner. Used instead of "pairing" or "team" wherever a sentence can carry it.

## The people

- **the four** — Dennis, Peter, Lindsay and Brooke. Named by first name everywhere on the page.
- **the two teams** — the two pairs that actually played the mixed games: Dennis + Brooke, and Peter + Lindsay; against Dennis + Lindsay, and Peter + Brooke. Exactly this and nothing more.

## What was measured

Each of the four has a value for each of five things, computed from the shot log. On the page they are never shown as numbers and never as a scale from weak to strong; a reader is told **where one of you sits among the four**.

- **attack** — how often a hard ball, once struck, is not returned.
- **defence** — how often a hard ball coming at you is answered without a miss.
- **positioning** — standing where the ball can reach you. The log sees only the court coordinates on the shots that carry them.
- **consistency** — how rarely you miss after the serve.
- **touch** — how rarely you miss a soft ball (dink, drop, lob).

Rank words, and nothing else in their place: **best**, **2nd**, **3rd**, **worst** — always "of the four". Not "strong", "strongest", "high", "score".

- **covers** — the things a pair is best at, or close to it, because one of the two is.
- **open** — a thing neither of the two is good at. Written "attack open", never "weakness".

## Games, rallies, scores

- **game** — one match in the log, named G1 … G7.
- **mixed game** — a game with one woman and one man on each side. Six of the seven.
- **same-gender game** — G5, Dennis + Peter against Brooke + Lindsay. The seventh. Not in the mixed numbers on the page.
- **rally** — one exchange: serve until the ball stops.
- **credits** — the only verb between the log and a pair. "The log credits this rally to Dennis + Brooke." The words *won*, *lost*, *beat*, *result*, *record* and *head-to-head* never appear about a rally.
- **score** — never shown, anywhere, for any game. The log's scores cannot be read: the winner label contradicts the last shot's side in 129 of 207 readable rallies, and the last shot carries no fault or winner field of its own.

## Unknowns

The page shows only what the log speaks to. Where it cannot, it says so once, in one phrase, and claims nothing.

- **not mixed** — this pair has never been on the court together in a mixed game. Dennis + Lindsay, Peter + Brooke: both played beside each other in the same-gender game, never in a mixed one.
- **not in our games** — the Dream Breaker. A real format, no game of it in the log.

There is no **guess**, no **projection**, no **estimate** and no **prediction** anywhere on the page: nothing is derived from individual attributes to stand in for a game nobody played.

## Sources

- **the shot log** — the per-shot record of the seven games. Every claim on the page traces to it.
- **the images** — the rally strips and the pair bars, drawn from the log at build time; the reader is never asked to read a number off them.
